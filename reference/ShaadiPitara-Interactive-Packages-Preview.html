Before you book

Feel clear before you say yes.

Booking your wedding team should feel simple. Here are the practical details, clearly laid out, so you know what to expect.

Ask us anything
Payment & booking terms
  • 40% of the amount is required to confirm the booking.
  • 40% is due during the first day of events.
  • The remaining 20% is payable once the deliverables are provided.
Travel, stay & meals
  • The client is responsible for accommodation, travel and meals for the team (2–3 members).
  • Accommodation is preferably arranged at the event property so the team can capture moments seamlessly.
Content posting & credits
  • All reels and stories include the Shaadi Pitara logo and proper credits.
  • Some content is posted from the official Shaadi Pitara account and other content from the couple's page.
  • Private content requests must be communicated in advance.
  • Share tagging preferences — who to tag or avoid — before the event.
Raw footage, delivery & additional notes
  • All raw and edited videos are stated as being delivered within 15–20 working days after the wedding.
  • Raw files are shared only after all final deliverables have been provided.
  • The client must provide a hard drive or pen drive for raw-footage transfer.
  • Add-on reels, extended coverage and date changes are chargeable.
  • Client consent is taken before sharing content with external collaborators.
  • The reference also states that deliverables are completed within 3–4 weeks after the event.
